# AI Policy

RepoLens welcomes AI-assisted contributions. Tools like Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, or Cursor can help you write code, tests, and docs. A person still has to stand behind every change.

## The rules

1. **Say so.** If you used AI to write any part of your pull request, tick the AI box in the [pull request template](.github/pull_request_template.md) and briefly say how you used it.
2. **Review it yourself.** Read, understand, and test every line before you open the PR. You should be able to explain any change when asked.
3. **You own it.** You are responsible for correctness and for making sure the contribution can be released under the project's MIT License.
4. **No low-effort or bulk PRs.** Please don't send unsolicited, mass-generated, or unreviewed AI pull requests. Open an issue first for anything larger than a small fix.

## What happens otherwise

Maintainers may close pull requests that ignore this policy without a detailed review.

Questions? Open an issue. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for the rest of the contribution guide.
